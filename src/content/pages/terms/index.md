## Scope

These terms cover your use of **agamesports.net**. They're separate from, and don't replace, the program-specific rules on our [Policies page](/policies/) or the requirements on our [Release Forms page](/release-forms/) — all three apply together once you register for a program.

## Using this site

You're welcome to browse this site, use the contact form, and download any release or medical form we've posted for your own use in registering a child for our programs. Please don't use automated tools to scrape or republish our content, and don't submit the contact form with false information or on someone else's behalf without their knowledge.

## Accuracy of information

We work to keep hours, pricing, and program details on this site accurate and current, but schedules and pricing can change between updates — especially seasonal camp dates. When in doubt, confirm details by phone at **(914) 278-9477** before you make plans around them.

## Program terms

Registering for a lesson, camp, party, or rental means you've also agreed to the policies covering that specific service — cancellation windows, refund rules, and package expiration are all on our [Policies page](/policies/), and required forms are on our [Release Forms page](/release-forms/).

## Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the site or our programs change. The version posted here is the current one — we'll note the effective date at the top of this page when it's meaningfully revised.
