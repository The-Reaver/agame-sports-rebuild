Not every kid needs a full pre-school program or a summer camp — sometimes what they need is one adult, one hour, focused on exactly where they're stuck. A-Game Academics runs that kind of support out of the same building as our sports programs, taught by educators, not coaches.

## Two formats

**1-on-1 tutoring** gives a student personalized attention with instruction and modifications matched to their specific grade level and needs — built for grade-level catch-up, homework help, or general skill-building.

**Quad Pods** are small four-week sessions with a 4-to-1 student-to-teacher ratio, one hour per week. Students still get plenty of individual attention from the teacher while working independently alongside a few peers in a classroom setting.

## What's covered

Reading, writing, mathematics, phonetics, and grammar — with the goal of helping kids build better grades, stronger reading and writing skills, and real test-taking strategy, not just get through homework.

## Led by

Mrs. Nicole Goodrich directs the program — a long-time early childhood educator and program director with more than 20 years across early childhood, elementary, and middle school settings.
