A pre-school built inside a sports facility sounds unusual until you see it running — real classrooms, a real curriculum, and a director with three decades in early childhood education, with a gym and turf right down the hall for when it's time to move.

## PK3 and PK4

**PK3** takes children from 22 months through 3½ years. **PK4** runs from 3½ to 5 years and requires students to be fully potty trained. Each classroom is staffed with a head teacher and an assistant teacher.

## What kids actually learn

The program is curriculum-based and structured, covering writing, phonics, and early mathematics alongside science, art, and technology. PK4 students work on numbers, three-dimensional shapes, phonics and blends, early writing, the alphabet, counting, and numeral recognition.

## Schedule options

Choose from **Full Day** (8:30am–3:00pm), **Half Day** (8:30am–12:00pm), or add **Extended Day** care (3:00–5:30pm, rate available on request) — Monday through Friday, running after Labor Day through June, with a summer session also available.
