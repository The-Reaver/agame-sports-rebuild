Six weeks, twice a week, in a small group with the same two instructors the whole way through — built to build both score and confidence before test day, not just cram content the week before.

## What the course covers

24 hours of instruction across Math and English, taught in small groups so students get real attention on their specific strengths and weaknesses. Every student receives a **Princeton Review SAT Prep** exam book, and the course includes **three full practice exams** administered along the way so progress is visible before the real test.

## Schedule

Most recently the course ran **Tuesdays and Thursdays, 4:00pm–6:00pm, March 16th through April 29th**, timed to finish ahead of the May 8th SAT date. Exact dates shift year to year with the College Board's test calendar — call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm the current session's dates.

## Who's teaching

**Geri Sheehan** teaches English — an 18-year English and Social Studies teacher and former writing specialist with experience in SAT/ACT test prep. **Siobhan Walsh** teaches Math — a Columbia University graduate with a master's degree and years of experience teaching test prep, including the SAT, across grades 6–12.

## Booking

The course runs in person at our 856 Main St facility. Call **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to reserve a seat before the session fills. Need broader academic support outside the SAT window? See [A-Game Academics](/school-programs/a-game-academics/).
