It surprises a lot of parents the first time they hear it: A-Game runs a full academic side of the building, not just sports. Our academic programs are directed by Nicole Goodrich, a career early-childhood educator, and staffed by teachers with real classroom backgrounds — not coaches filling a gap in the schedule.

## What we offer

**[Little Learners Pre-School](/school-programs/little-learners/)** is our year-round pre-school academy for ages 22 months through 5 years, with full-day, half-day, and extended-day options built around a real curriculum — writing, phonics, early math, science, art, and technology.

**[A-Game Academics](/school-programs/a-game-academics/)** covers 1-on-1 tutoring and small "Quad Pod" group sessions for students of every grade level, focused on reading, writing, math, and general academic support.

**[SAT Prep Program](/school-programs/sat-prep-program/)** is a structured, in-person course for high schoolers, taught by two veteran educators, built to build both scores and confidence ahead of test day.

## Not sure which fits?

If your child is under 5, start with Little Learners. If they're already in school and need help keeping up or catching up, A-Game Academics is the flexible option. If they're staring down the SAT, our prep course runs on a set schedule tied to the spring test date — call **(914) 278-9477** to check timing.
