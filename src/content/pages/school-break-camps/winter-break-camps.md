Presidents' Week is one of the longer winter breaks on the school calendar — long enough that a week of unstructured downtime turns into a week of screens. A-Game runs a full slate of winter camps to fill that week with real activity, split across enough tracks that most ages find a fit.

## Pick your track

**Multi-Sport Camp** covers soccer, wiffleball, dodgeball, kickball, flag football, obstacle courses, capture the flag, and more.

**Little Athletes Multi-Sport Camp** (half day) is built for our youngest campers, who must be fully potty trained, with soccer, t-ball, dodgeball, kickball, football, obstacle courses, and music.

**Little Learners Sports and Academics Camp** (full day) also requires campers be fully potty trained, and blends academic lessons, snack, lunch, rest time, music, arts, and science with the same sports rotation.

**Basketball Camp** (ages 6–14) builds fundamental skills — ball handling, defense, footwork, conditioning.

**Speed Training Mini Camp** (ages 10–15) is a 4-day, small-group program capped at 8 kids per session, covering plyometrics, agility drills, and timed sprints.

**Baseball & Softball Mini Camp** covers hitting drills, fielding, pitching mechanics, and conditioning — spots are limited.

**Volleyball Camp** (ages 8–14) covers setting, passing, hitting, serving, digging, and blocking.
