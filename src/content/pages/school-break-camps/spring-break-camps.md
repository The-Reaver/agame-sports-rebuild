Spring break is a full week off, not just a day — which means you actually get to pick the right fit instead of taking whatever's available. A-Game runs six different Spring Break camp tracks under one roof, so siblings of different ages (or interests) can all camp at the same place, at the same time.

## Pick your track

**Multi-Sport Camp** rotates through soccer, wiffleball, dodgeball, kickball, flag football, obstacle courses, capture the flag, and more camp games — full-week variety for kids who like a little of everything.

**Little Athletes Multi-Sport Camp** is our half-day version built for younger campers, with soccer, t-ball, dodgeball, kickball, football, obstacle courses, and music.

**Little Learners Sports & Academics Camp** is a full-day option that pairs real classroom time — academic lessons, arts, science — with rest time and sports, structured like a school day rather than a straight sports camp.

**Spring Break Baseball & Softball Camp** covers throwing, pitching, tee work, infield and outfield defense, base running, and hitting. Campers need to bring their own bat, helmet, and glove.

**Spring Break Basketball Camp** (ages 6–14) is built for boys and girls of every skill level, with a low player-to-instructor ratio, covering speed, ball handling, defense, form, passing, and conditioning.

**Spring Break Volleyball Camp** is built to make your camper fundamentally sound in the game from the ground up.

## Ages and pricing

Age ranges and pricing vary by track and by year — Basketball is confirmed for ages 6–14, but other tracks don't publish a fixed age range on our site. Call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm the right track for your camper's age and current pricing.

## Before camp week

A completed medical form is required before the first day of any camp — see our [policies page](/policies/) for cancellation terms, makeup sessions, and what to submit.
