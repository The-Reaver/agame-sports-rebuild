School gives your kid a random Monday off and suddenly you're rearranging your whole week. A-Game's School Holiday Camps are built for exactly that — single days or short stretches, run by A-Game Sports coaches, so your player gets a day of real activity instead of a day parked in front of a screen.

## What a holiday camp day looks like

Camps rotate through soccer, wiffleball, dodgeball, kickball, Gaga, basketball, flag football, obstacle courses, capture the flag, and other camp games — high-energy, all-sport variety rather than a single-sport grind. Our youngest campers get an age-appropriate version with soccer, t-ball, dodgeball, kickball, football, obstacle courses, and music built in.

Camps run on individual school-closure days scattered across the calendar — Election Day, Veterans Day, Columbus Day, MLK Day, and single-day or two-day stretches in late spring have all appeared on past schedules. Because these dates depend on your school district's calendar, call **(914) 278-9477** or check our [calendar](/calendar/) for the next one coming up.

## Ages

Multi-Sport camps run for ages 6–12; a separate Little Athletes version is built for ages 3–5. Both are led by A-Game Sports coaches at our 856 Main St facility.

## Before camp day

Registration is handled online for each date, and required medical forms are the same across all our camps — see our [policies page](/policies/) for what's needed before your camper's first day. Looking for a longer stretch off instead of a single day? See our [Spring Break Camps](/school-break-camps/spring-break-camps/) and [Winter Break Camps](/school-break-camps/winter-break-camps/).
