Fall is our busiest flag football season, with instructional scrimmages and leagues organized by grade level so every kid is playing against — and with — peers at their stage.

## What's running this fall

- **K–2nd Grade** Instructional Scrimmages
- **3rd–5th Grade** Outdoor Scrimmages, Speed & Agility
- **3rd–8th Grade** Instructional Scrimmages
- **3rd–5th & 6th–8th Grade, Girls-Only** Outdoor Scrimmages
- **Girls 2nd–4th Grade** Instructional Scrimmages
- **New this year:** a Girls Flag Football Modified, JV, Varsity, and Travel Training Program
- **Quarterback Skills Training** for players who want to focus on the position
- An **Outdoor Flag Football League** for kids ready for full-game reps
- **Little Athletes Flag Football** for our youngest players (see [Little Athletes Academy](/little-athletes-academy-programs/))

## Dates, times, and pricing

Exact start dates, weekly schedules, and pricing are set fresh each fall and vary by program. Call **(914) 278-9477** or reach out through the contact form to find the right fit for your player's grade and confirm current pricing.
