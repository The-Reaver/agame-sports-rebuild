Not every adult who trains here is in a program — some just want a place to work out on their own time. A-Game's recreational gym membership gives you access to the space itself, no class schedule or coach required.

## Who it's for

Membership is open to ages 16 and older.

## Pricing

Choose the commitment level that fits: **$50/month**, **$25/week**, or **$10/day**.

## Access hours

Recreational gym access runs **Monday–Thursday 11:00am–4:00pm**, **Friday 12:30–4:00pm**, and **Saturday–Sunday 10:00am–5:00pm** — a separate window from our general facility hours, so plan around it.

## Signing up

Space is limited on a given day, so call ahead. Reach us at **(914) 278-9477** or through our [contact form](/contact/) to check availability and get started.
