Most corporate outings ask people to sit through another agenda. Ours puts your team on turf instead — competing, sweating a little, and remembering it by Monday. A-Game's 25,000-square-foot facility has enough range to run a full team-building day or just an afternoon activity break, with meeting space if you need to fold in actual work.

## What your team can do here

Soccer, flag football, dodgeball, 9-inning baseball games, relay races, obstacle courses, a home run derby on our HitTrax batting cages, group yoga, ping pong tournaments, European handball, and other team-building games — mixed and matched to fit your group.

## The space

The facility includes a corporate meeting room, private rooms, turf fields, batting cages, a recreational area, and a strength & conditioning area, so an event can move between competition and a sit-down segment without leaving the building.

## What's included

A-Game staff and coaches run the activities, and the facility provides complimentary high-speed internet and complimentary parking. Catered meal options and business presentation materials are available on request.

Pricing depends on group size, activities, and how much of the facility you need. Call **(914) 278-9477** or use our [contact form](/contact/) to talk through your group and get a quote.
