Nobody wants to run a birthday party and be the party at the same time. Ours are built so the parents get to actually be guests — our coaches lead the games, our staff handles the pizza and paper goods, and you show up to celebrate instead of manage.

## Packages

**Classic — $595.** Up to 15 kids (additional kids $27 each). Includes 60 minutes of turf play with up to three activity choices, 30 minutes in a private party room, two party coaches plus a reception host, and pizza (4 pies), juice, water, tablecloths, paper goods, and candles. Ask about our current summer promotion — select July and August dates are sometimes available at a reduced rate if booked early enough in the year.

**All-Star — $750.** Everything in Classic, plus sports bottles filled with candy for every guest. Additional kids are $30 each.

**Just Play Classic — $250.** For families who just want the turf time without the food and room — up to 8 kids (additional kids $20 each), 90 minutes of turf play, two coaches, no room or food included.

**Just Play All-Star — $350.** Just Play Classic plus the candy-filled sports bottles.

## What the kids actually do

Coaches run wiffleball, soccer, kickball, dodgeball, capture the flag, relay races, tug-of-war, tag games, and flag football — mixed to fit the group's age and energy. Want to add a bounce house, an inflatable obstacle course, or a gaga pit? Ask about add-ons when you book; each carries its own fee.

## Booking details

Parties run **Friday 5:00–8:00pm** and **Saturday–Sunday 11:00am–5:00pm**. A non-refundable deposit locks in your date — $275 for Classic packages, $150 for Just Play. Need to reschedule? Give us at least 48 hours' notice. Guest counts running over the package limit (22+ for Classic, 8+ for Just Play) may add a $150 additional-space fee.
