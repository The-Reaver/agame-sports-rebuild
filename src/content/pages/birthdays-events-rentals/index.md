A-Game isn't just where your kid trains — it's also where New Rochelle comes to celebrate. We run indoor birthday parties on our turf, take the party to your backyard or local park with our outdoor package, rent out individual spaces by the hour, and host corporate teams looking for something better than a conference room. There's also a standing gym membership if you just want regular access to the space.

## Figuring out which one you need

If you're planning a kid's birthday and want everything handled in one building — turf time, a private room, pizza, coaches running games — start with **[Birthday Parties](/birthdays-events-rentals/birthday-parties/)**. If your backyard, a local park, or another spot works better for your crew, **[Outdoor Birthday Parties](/birthdays-events-rentals/outdoor-birthday-parties/)** brings the coaches and equipment to you.

Need a specific space — a batting cage, half the turf field, the party room — without the full party package? **[Facility Rentals](/birthdays-events-rentals/facility-rentals/)** lets you book by the hour. Planning something for work instead of a birthday? **[Corporate Retreats](/birthdays-events-rentals/corporate-retreats/)** turns our 25,000-square-foot facility into a team-building venue with catering options and meeting space. And if you just want to use the gym on your own schedule, look at **[Gym Membership](/birthdays-events-rentals/gym-membership/)**.
