Sometimes the party belongs at home, not at our facility — a backyard, the neighborhood park, a field near you. Our coaches bring the games and sanitized equipment to wherever you are and run the same high-energy party you'd get on our turf.

## Packages

**Classic Outdoor — $375/hour.** Outdoor play led by our coaches, with sanitized equipment. Covers 60+ minutes of sports play with a choice of up to three activities for a base group of 15 kids (additional kids are $20 each). Two coaches run the party.

**All-Star add-on — +$100.** Adds paper goods and setup.

**MVP add-on — +$150.** Adds branded sports bottles filled with candy for guests.

Optional cupcakes or a cake can be added for an extra cost.

## Activities

Wiffleball, soccer, kickball, dodgeball, an obstacle course, relay races, tug-of-war, and flag football — customizable to the group.

## Where it works

Your backyard, a local park or field, or any other spot large enough for sports play. Locations within 5 miles of our facility need a 90-minute minimum booking. Between 5 and 10 miles, plan on a 2-hour minimum plus a $75 travel fee. Beyond 10 miles, call to discuss pricing.

## Available times

**Weekdays 4:00–8:00pm** and **weekends 11:00am–5:00pm**, with other times available on request.

## Booking

A $100 non-refundable deposit reserves your date. Call **(914) 278-9477**, reach out to **Darin Feldman** or **Kevin Plein**, or use our [contact form](/contact/) to get started.
