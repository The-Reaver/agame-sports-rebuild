Not every reason to use A-Game is a full program or a party. Sometimes you just need the space — a full team practice on the turf, a couple hours in the cage before tryouts, a quiet room for a meeting. Facility rentals let you book by the hour, either as a one-off or a standing weekly slot.

## Spaces available

Full soccer field, half field, infield, infield tunnel, batting tunnels (with or without HitTrax), batting cages (with or without a pitching machine), the strength & conditioning area, the basketball area, the ping pong table, and the party room.

## Pricing

Hourly rates run **$15–$225** depending on the space — a half field runs $150/hour, a batting tunnel with HitTrax runs $90/hour, and the party room runs $70–$80/hour. All rates are subject to change based on season, time of day, and availability, so confirm current pricing when you call.

## Capacity notes

Adult evening soccer rentals are capped at 12 players per full field. Batting cage rentals are limited to 3 people inside the cage itself (spectators are fine outside it).

## Hours & booking

Rentals run within our regular facility hours — **Monday–Friday 9:00am–10:00pm** and **Saturday–Sunday 8:00am–7:00pm**, with summer hours varying and the facility generally closed Sundays outside of special events. Book a walk-in slot or a recurring weekly rental by calling **(914) 278-9477**, based on current availability.
