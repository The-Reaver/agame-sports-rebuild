Fall is when Little Athletes Academy runs its widest range of classes — nineteen different offerings, sorted by age, so a 10-month-old and a kindergarten-bound 5-year-old both land in the right room.

## What's running this fall

- **Mommy & Me** — ages 10–24 months
- **Music & Movement**, **Incredible Inflatable Fridays** — ages 1–3
- **Soccer Littles**, **Multi-Sport Minis**, **PLAY PLAY PLAY** — ages 1½–3
- **Combo Class: Sports & Art** — ages 2–3
- **Pee Wee Football** — ages 2–4
- **Micro Soccer**, **Micro Golf**, **T-Ball Instructional League**, **Soccer Instructional League** — ages 3–5
- **Multi-Sport MADNESS** — ages 3–6
- **T-Ball**, **Basketball on the Court**, **Lacrosse**, **Pre-K Flag Football**, **Volley Tots**, **Dodgeball & Field Games** — ages 4–5

## Dates, times, and pricing

Exact start dates, weekly schedules, and pricing are set fresh each fall and vary by class. Call **(914) 278-9477** or reach out through the contact form to find the right class for your child's age and confirm current pricing.
