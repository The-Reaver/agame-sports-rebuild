Not every player wants — or needs — a travel commitment. But for the ones who do, A-Game fields competitive teams and club programs across several sports, coached by the same staff who run our lesson and camp programs.

## Programs

- **[Westchester Fire Travel Baseball](/baseball-programs/fire-travel-baseball-teams/)** — tournament-level travel baseball teams.
- **Westchester Heat Softball** — travel softball teams competing outside our regular season programs.
- **[Travel/Tournament Co-Ed Flag Football](/football-programs/travel-flag-football/)** — a more competitive flag football option beyond our house leagues.
- **Club Field Hockey** — a travel-level field hockey program.
- **Club Volleyball** — a travel-level volleyball program.
- **Baseball Travel Ready Training Program** — preparatory training built for players aiming for a travel roster spot.

Rosters, tryout timing, age eligibility, and pricing vary by sport and season — call **(914) 278-9477** to ask about the current status of any specific team.

## Is travel the right fit?

If your player is already comfortable with the fundamentals and wants more competitive reps and a bigger commitment, a travel or club team is worth a conversation. If they're still building basics, our sport-specific lesson and camp programs are the better starting point — travel coaches are happy to point you toward the right one.
