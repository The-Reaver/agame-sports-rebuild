Mornings at Flowers Field built around real touches on the ball — dribbling, ball control, passing, shooting, and defense — plus position-specific technique work, so every camper leaves each week having actually improved, not just scrimmaged for three hours.

## Every skill level, one field

Coaches run this camp for beginners and advanced players alike, keeping a low player-to-instructor ratio so nobody gets lost whether it's their first summer with a soccer ball or their fifth.

## Weeks and pricing

Weekly sessions run **$275/week** for registrations after April 1. Early registrants don't have to lock in which specific weeks they're attending right away — ask about the flexible-week policy when you call to register.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** must be on file before your camper's first day — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit it. Exact week dates are posted each spring; call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's calendar and available weeks.
