Three evenings, capped at 6 campers, built for beginners learning the windmill pitching motion from the ground up. Every 90-minute session runs the same structure — dynamic warm-up, pitching drills, body strength exercises, and a cool-down stretch — so mechanics get reinforced, not just introduced once and forgotten.

## Built for beginners

This bootcamp is explicitly for beginners, not a tune-up for pitchers who already have a motion. Small group size means coaches can watch and correct every camper's mechanics across all three days, not just call out a lineup.

## Sessions and pricing

A single 3-day session is **$200**. Book two for **$185/week ($370 total)**, or three for **$167/week ($500 total)** — multi-session discounts require calling **(914) 278-9477** to register directly.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** and **immunization record** must be on file before your camper's first session — see [policies](/policies/) for cancellation and makeup-session details. Exact session dates are posted each spring; call to confirm this year's calendar.
