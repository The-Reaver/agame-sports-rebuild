A flag football camp run entirely for girls — same real position instruction as our co-ed camp, in a group where every camper is a girl on the field. Coaches teach quarterback, wide receiver, defensive back, and linebacker fundamentals in the first half of each day, then move into scrimmage games once the group has the drills down.

## Why a girls-only group

Some campers play their best football in a group of just girls — more reps, more confidence speaking up, less hesitation to go all-in on a drill. This camp gives that option without changing the level of instruction: same non-contact fundamentals, same position-specific coaching, same daily and weekly prizes.

## Weeks and pricing

Book by the week: **$300** for one week, down to **$250/week ($750 total)** across three weeks.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** and **immunization record** must be on file before your camper's first session — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit both. Exact week dates are posted each spring; call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's calendar.
