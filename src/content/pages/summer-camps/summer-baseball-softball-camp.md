This camp runs on stations, not standing in line. Campers rotate through throwing, pitching, tee work, infield and outfield defense, base running, and situational play — the same fundamentals we teach in our year-round lessons, just stretched across a full week at Flowers Field.

## Why the ratio matters

At 5 campers per counselor, no kid spends the week waiting their turn at the back of a long line. Coaches see enough reps from each camper to actually correct mechanics in real time — and campers leave with weekly prizes and awards recognizing real improvement, not just participation.

## What to bring

Campers need their own **glove, bat, and helmet** — the one piece of gear we ask families to supply so equipment always fits right. Pack water, lunch, and snacks daily; see our [policies page](/policies/) for our allergy and lunch guidelines (the facility is not peanut-free).

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** (physician-signed) must be on file before your camper's first session — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit it. Exact week dates are confirmed each spring; call **(914) 278-9477** to lock in your weeks before they fill.
