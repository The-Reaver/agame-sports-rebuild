Summer at A-Game runs on two tracks: **broad, multi-sport camps** for kids who want to try everything, and **focused, sport-specific bootcamps** for players who already know what they want to get better at.

## For younger and first-time campers

**[Little Athletes Multi-Sport Summer Camp](/summer-camps/little-athletes-multi-sport-summer-camp/)** and **[Little Learners](/summer-camps/little-learners/)** are built for kids just starting to explore sports — age-appropriate stations, short attention-span-friendly rotations, and coaches trained specifically for younger campers.

## For sport-specific training

Baseball and softball players have the **[Summer Baseball & Softball Camp](/summer-camps/summer-baseball-softball-camp/)** at Flowers Field, plus focused options like the **[Summer Hitters Bootcamp](/summer-camps/summer-hitters-bootcamp/)** and **[Mini Windmill Pitching Bootcamp](/summer-camps/mini-windmill-pitching-bootcamp/)**. Basketball, soccer, volleyball, and flag football each have their own dedicated camp weeks too — see the full list below.

## Booking a week

Exact week dates are set each spring and posted on this page and by phone — call **(914) 278-9477** and we'll confirm this year's calendar, availability, and multi-week pricing for the camp you're looking at.
