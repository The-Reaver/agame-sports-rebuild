Three evenings built around one goal: a better swing. This bootcamp targets mechanical swing issues, timing, and hitting efficiency through hand-eye coordination drills, situational hitting, tee work, weighted-ball training, front toss, and live pitching — with **HitTrax** data giving campers and coaches real numbers on exit velocity and contact quality, not just a coach's eye.

## What to bring

Players need their own **bat and helmet** — the gear has to fit right, so we ask families to supply it.

## Sessions and pricing

A single 3-day session is **$195**. Book two for **$180/week ($360 total)**, or three for **$165/week ($495 total)**.

## Before the first day

Medical forms are required before your camper's first session — see [policies](/policies/) for full details on what's needed and how to submit it. Exact session dates are posted each spring; call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's calendar.
