Four indoor evenings, one small group, no bleachers full of kids waiting their turn. This mini camp keeps sessions capped at 6 campers so every camper gets real reps at receiving, route running, passing, defending receivers, and field positioning — the fundamentals, taught the right way, early.

## Why indoors, why small

Running this camp inside A-Game's own facility means weather never cancels a session, and capping it at 6 campers per session means coaches can actually coach — correcting a route or a catch technique in the moment instead of shouting instructions across a crowd. Camp closes with skills competitions and prizes so campers leave with something to show for the week.

## Sessions and pricing

A single 4-day session runs **$185**. Sign up for two sessions and the rate drops to **$175 per week ($350 total)** — but that discount only applies if you **call (914) 278-9477** to register directly rather than booking online.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** must be on file before your camper's first session — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit it, and [policies](/policies/) for cancellation and makeup-session details. Exact session dates are posted each spring; call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's calendar and lock in a spot before sessions fill.
