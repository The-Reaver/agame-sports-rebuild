A full day on the court, every day of the week. Campers work speed, ball handling, defense, shooting form, passing, angles, conditioning, footwork, and explosiveness — the same building blocks whether your camper is picking up a basketball for the first time or already playing travel ball.

## Built for every level

Coaches group activity by skill level so beginners aren't lost and advanced campers still get pushed, all under a low player-to-instructor ratio that keeps kids engaged for the full 9am–3pm day rather than standing around.

## Location note

This camp runs off-site at **Holy Trinity Greek Orthodox Church**, 10 Mill Road, New Rochelle — not at the A-Game facility on Main Street. Plan drop-off and pickup accordingly.

## Weeks and pricing

Book by the week and the per-week rate drops the more weeks you add: **$475** for one week, down to **$415/week** across four weeks ($1,660 total).

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** must be on file before your camper's first day — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit it. Exact week dates are posted each spring; call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's calendar.
