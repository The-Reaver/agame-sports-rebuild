A morning camp built entirely around ages 3 to 5 — not a scaled-down version of the big-kid camps. Little Athletes rotates through soccer, wiffleball, dodgeball, kickball, flag football, obstacle courses, and camp games, so the youngest campers get to try a little of everything without any one sport asking too much focus.

## Why the ratio matters this young

At a 5-to-1 camper-to-counselor ratio, every camper gets seen — for a 3-year-old, that's the difference between a fun morning and a rough one. Counselors know each kid's name, energy level, and attention span, not just their spot in a rotation.

## Weeks and pricing

Ten weeks run across the summer, and pricing steps down the more weeks you book: from **$350** for a single week down to **$3,050** across all ten weeks (roughly $305/week at the top tier). See [policies](/policies/) for the pro-rated credit on any 4-day holiday week.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** must be on file before your camper's first day — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit it. Exact week dates are posted each spring; call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's calendar.
