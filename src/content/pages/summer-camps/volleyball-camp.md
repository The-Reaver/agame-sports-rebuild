A full day of volleyball fundamentals — setting, passing, hitting, serving, digging, and blocking — worked through rotational stations so every camper touches every skill, not just the ones they're already good at. Competitive gameplay and prizes close out the week.

## Location note

This camp runs off-site at **Holy Trinity Greek Orthodox Church**, 10 Mill Road, New Rochelle — not at the A-Game facility on Main Street. Plan drop-off and pickup accordingly.

## Weeks and pricing

**$475** for one week, or **$425/week ($850 total)** for two weeks. A low player-to-instructor ratio keeps every camper involved across the full 9am–3pm day.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** and **immunization record** must be on file before your camper's first day — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit both. Exact week dates are posted each spring; call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's calendar.
