A rotating series of 4-day evening mini camps through the summer, each one grouping campers into a tight age band so the work fits the kid. This isn't sport-specific skill training — it's the athletic base underneath every sport: strength, speed, and agility work that carries over to whatever your camper plays next.

## How the sessions are grouped

Roughly ten sessions run across the summer, each with its own age band (bands shift slightly week to week, generally landing somewhere in the 7–14 range). Because the exact age cutoffs move from week to week, call **(914) 278-9477** to find the session that matches your camper's age.

## Pricing

Each 4-day session runs **$175 or $195**, depending on the week. Call to confirm which rate applies to a specific session.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** and **immunization record** must be on file before your camper's first session — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit both. Exact session dates are posted each spring; call to confirm this year's calendar.
