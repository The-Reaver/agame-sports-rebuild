A full 9-to-3 day for ages 3 to 5 that doesn't ask a little kid to choose between running around and sitting down to learn — Little Learners does both. Mornings mix in sports like soccer, wiffleball, dodgeball, kickball, flag football, and obstacle courses; the day rounds out with art, science, music, quiet time, math, and phonics.

## A full day, built for the age

This is a longer day than our other 3–5 camps (9:00am–3:00pm), which makes it a real childcare option for the summer — not just a morning activity. Counselors balance active play with structured quiet time so a full day doesn't overwhelm a preschooler.

## Weeks and pricing

Multiple weeks are available across the summer, and pricing steps down the more you book: from **$495** for one week to roughly **$475/week** at three weeks and lower still at higher week counts. Call **(914) 278-9477** for this year's exact per-week rate at each tier.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** and **immunization record** must be on file before your camper's first day — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit both. Exact week dates are posted each spring; call to confirm this year's calendar.
