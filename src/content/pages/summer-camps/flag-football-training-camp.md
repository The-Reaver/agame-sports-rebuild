Mornings at Flowers Field split into two halves: fundamental, non-contact skill work first, then scrimmage games once campers can run the drills clean. Coaches run position-specific instruction for quarterback, wide receiver, defensive back, and linebacker so every camper — not just the fastest kid — has a job to do on the field.

## How the week runs

The first half of each day is all fundamentals: routes, coverage, footwork, the rules of the game. The second half is game time, with daily and weekly prizes for standout effort — not just outcome.

## Pricing

Pricing runs **$300–$350 per week** depending on the session — the opening session is a shorter 4-day week at the lower rate. Book two weeks together and the rate drops to **$325/week ($650 total)**. Call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's exact rates and available sessions.

## Before the first day

A completed **medical form** and **immunization record** must be on file before your camper's first session — see [release forms](/release-forms/) for how to submit both. Exact week dates are posted each spring; call to confirm this year's calendar and reserve a spot before it fills.
