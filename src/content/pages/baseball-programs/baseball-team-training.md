Some things are hard to drill without a full team on the field — cutoffs, relays, defensive alignment. A-Game's team training sessions bring your whole roster into our indoor facility to work on exactly that, whether you're getting ready for a season or sharpening things up mid-season.

## What a session covers

Sessions flex to whatever your roster needs most that week — tightening up cutoffs and relays, addressing a defensive weak spot, or working strength and conditioning in alongside baseball skill work. Every player gets more reps, not just your top of the lineup, and the team builds camaraderie in the process.

## Who this is for

Team training is built for rostered teams — travel squads like **[Westchester Fire](/baseball-programs/fire-travel-baseball-teams/)**, school teams, or Little League teams looking for extra indoor practice time during the off-season.

## Booking

Pricing and session length depend on your team's size and the focus of the session — call us at **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to talk through what your team needs and get a quote. See our [policies page](/policies/) for cancellation and makeup session terms.
