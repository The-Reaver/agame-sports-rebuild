Westchester Fire is our competitive travel baseball program, sponsored by A-Game Sports and built around the same indoor facility our lesson players train in every day, with year-round access to HitTrax analytics. The coaching philosophy is simple: **positive reinforcement, not humiliation.**

## Ages and season fees

Fire fields teams from **8U through 16U**. Season fees run **$875 for 8U, $925 for 9U-12U, and $975 for 13U-16U** (uniforms are purchased separately). The age cutoff for each division is **May 1st**.

## Tryouts

- **8U and 9U** hold group tryouts, $30 registration fee
- **10U through 16U** try out privately, $50 fee
- Each player attends one tryout session

Call **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form for current tryout dates and open roster spots.

## What a season looks like

Teams play three seasons a year:

| Season | Games | Practices | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Summer | 12–20 | Weekly, 90 min | 1 tournament (10U and up) |
| Fall | 12–20 | Weekly, 90 min | 1 tournament; winter workouts required |
| Spring | 12–20 | Twice weekly, 90 min | Memorial Day tournament |

Fire teams compete in the GHVBL league in addition to tournament play. **Required winter workouts** run $595-$675 depending on age group. Optional add-ons include pitcher/catcher labs ($295) and strength and conditioning ($175-$675 depending on age).

## How Fire connects to our lesson programs

Many Fire players build their foundation in our **[1-on-1 lessons](/baseball-programs/baseball-1-on-1-lessons/)** or **[small group lessons](/baseball-programs/baseball-small-group-lessons/)** before trying out. Once rostered, teams can also book **[team training](/baseball-programs/baseball-team-training/)** blocks between practices.
