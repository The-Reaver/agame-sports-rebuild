Some players don't need a broad overhaul — they need concentrated, repeated reps on the swing itself. The Rapid Fire Hitting Package is 10 one-on-one, 30-minute lessons focused entirely on **swing mechanics, bat speed, and offensive output**, with an experienced hitting coach every time.

## What's included

- **10 individual 30-minute hitting lessons**, one-on-one with a hitting coach
- Instructors assigned based on availability at time of scheduling
- Open to both baseball and softball hitters

## The fine print

This package moves fast on purpose: all 10 lessons must be used within **45 days of purchase**, and the package can't be shared across family members — it's built for one player to see quick, compounding results. See our [policies page](/policies/) for makeup session and medical form details.
