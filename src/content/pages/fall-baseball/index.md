Spring and summer aren't the only seasons that matter. A-Game's fall baseball lineup is our biggest of the year, keeping players sharp indoors while outdoor fields go quiet, so nobody starts next spring from scratch.

## This fall's programs

- **Hitting University** — dedicated hitting instruction
- **Baseball Beginnings** — introductory instruction for players new to the game
- **5 Tool Player Series** — well-rounded development across hitting, fielding, throwing, speed, and power
- **Build-A-Pitcher Series** — focused pitching mechanics and development
- **Gold Glove Fielding Program** — defensive skill-building
- **2-on-1 Baseball Hitting Program** — small-format hitting instruction
- **Saturday Sluggers** and **Sunday Sluggers** — weekend hitting sessions
- **Baseball Travel Ready Training Program** — preparation for travel-team tryouts
- **Catcher's Series** — position-specific instruction for catchers
- **Outdoor Gold Glove Fielding Program** — defensive work outdoors while weather allows
- **6 Week Outdoor Little League Training Series** — Little League-focused outdoor training
- **Cage Circuit Training Series** — batting cage-based conditioning and reps
- **Modified JV and Varsity Baseball Spring Training** — offseason training for modified, JV, and varsity players

Each series runs as its own block of sessions; a player can register for one or stack a few, depending on age, level, and what they want to work on.

## Ages, pricing, and exact dates

Fall series dates, age breakdowns, and pricing are set closer to each season and can shift year to year — call us at **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to get the current fall schedule and reserve a spot before a series fills.

## Looking for more individual attention?

If your player wants dedicated one-on-one time instead of (or alongside) a fall series, take a look at our **[1-on-1 lessons](/baseball-programs/baseball-1-on-1-lessons/)** or **[small group lessons](/baseball-programs/baseball-small-group-lessons/)**.
