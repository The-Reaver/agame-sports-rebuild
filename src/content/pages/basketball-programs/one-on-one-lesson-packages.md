A full hour with one coach means no waiting your turn and no split attention — just your player and an instructor working through exactly what's holding their game back, whether that's shooting form, ballhandling under pressure, or footwork on defense.

## Session lengths and packages

| Package | Session length | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 1 lesson | 60 minutes | $140 |
| 5 lessons | 60 minutes each | $115/session ($575 total) |
| 10 lessons | 60 minutes each | $100/session ($1,000 total) |

The more sessions you book at once, the lower the per-session rate. Multi-lesson packages need to be selected in full at registration — see our [policies page](/policies/) for expiration windows and cancellation terms.

## Schedule

Lessons run **Thursdays**, either **5:00–6:00pm** or **6:00–7:00pm**.
