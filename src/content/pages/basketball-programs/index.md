Basketball at A-Game doesn't stop when the outdoor courts get cold. Our indoor courts keep New Rochelle players working on ballhandling, shooting, and game IQ straight through the year, with coaching built around each player's actual level.

## How players train with us

**[1-on-1 Lesson Packages](/basketball-programs/one-on-one-lesson-packages/)** put a coach's full attention on your player's specific game — footwork, shot mechanics, or reading the floor — a full hour at a time. When the leaves turn, our **[Fall Basketball](/fall-basketball/)** programs pick up with structured seasonal play, including the **[Nothing But Net 3-on-3 Tournament](/fall-basketball/fall-outdoor-nothing-but-net-3-on-3-basketball-tournament/)**, an outdoor bracket-style event for competitive teams.

## Not sure where to start?

If your player wants focused, individual skill work, a 1-on-1 lesson package is the fastest way to get a coach's eyes on exactly what needs fixing. If they're ready to compete against other kids their age, ask us about our fall lineup — it spans instructional scrimmages, skill-building training, and a girls-only program. Call **(914) 278-9477** for what's currently open.
