## What this site collects

This website collects information in two places:

- **The contact form** (on our [Contact page](/contact/)): name, email address, phone number, your message, and your preferred contact method.
- **Program and camp registration**, which we currently handle by phone, email, and paper/PDF forms rather than through this website — see [Release Forms](/release-forms/) for what that process involves.

We do not run an online store or payment checkout on this site, so we never collect card or payment information here.

## Information about children

Some of what we collect is *about* a child — a name, age, or medical note on a release or medical form — provided by a parent or legal guardian as part of registering that child for a program. We only collect what a given program actually requires to run safely (for example, camp medical forms exist because camps legally and practically need them). We do not use information about a child for marketing, and we do not knowingly collect information directly from a child browsing this site.

If you're a parent with questions about what information a specific program requires, or you'd like a form or record corrected or removed, contact us at [info@agamesports.net](mailto:info@agamesports.net) or **(914) 278-9477**.

## Cookies and analytics

As of this site's launch, agamesports.net does not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking analytics. If that changes in the future — for example, to add basic, privacy-respecting traffic analytics — this page will be updated first, and any required consent banner will be added at that time.

## Third-party links

This site links out to third-party platforms we use directly, including Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter/X, and to downloadable PDF forms. Those platforms have their own privacy practices, separate from ours — we encourage you to review them before sharing information there.

## How to reach us about privacy

Questions, corrections, or removal requests can go to [info@agamesports.net](mailto:info@agamesports.net) or **(914) 278-9477**. We'll respond directly rather than through an automated system.
