A-Game Sports sits at 856 Main St in New Rochelle, right off I-95 (Exit 15), with parking right at the door. Inside is 25,000 square feet of indoor training space — no waiting on weather, no losing a season to winter.

## What's inside

**Multi-sport turf fields.** A full turfed soccer field that splits into a half field, giving us the flexibility to run a full-team scrimmage or two smaller sessions side by side.

**Baseball & softball setup.** An infield and infield tunnel for defensive reps, plus batting tunnels — some equipped with HitTrax technology for real swing and exit-velocity data — and batting cages available with or without a pitching machine.

**Strength & conditioning area.** A dedicated space for speed, strength, and conditioning work, separate from the sport-specific fields and cages.

**Basketball court area** and a **lacrosse wall** for individual reps between team sessions.

**Ping pong table** and a **party room** for the lighter side of things — birthday parties, team hangouts, and downtime between sessions.

## Hours

**Monday–Friday:** 9:00am – 10:00pm
**Saturday–Sunday:** 8:00am – 7:00pm

Additional hours are available by request, and summer hours vary. The facility is closed Sundays except for birthday parties and special events — call ahead to confirm.
