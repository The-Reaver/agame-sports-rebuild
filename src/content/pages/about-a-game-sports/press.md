Since opening our doors in New Rochelle in 2013, A-Game Sports has been covered by local and regional outlets — usually around the story of two founders who left finance careers to build a 25,000-square-foot indoor sports facility, and the fact that it lets kids train through every New Rochelle winter instead of losing months to weather.

## Where we've been covered

Fios1 News, News 12 Westchester, Pix11 News, North Avenue Nation TV, The Daily Voice, Talk of the Sound, Westchester Business Journal, Westchester Family Magazine, New York MetroParents, Mommy Poppins, The Pelham Post, and Hamlet Hub have all featured A-Game Sports over the years.

## Notable visits

Yankees legend **Mariano Rivera** stopped by our baseball camp, and former Mets center fielder **Mookie Wilson** worked with our campers directly — two names our baseball kids still bring up.
