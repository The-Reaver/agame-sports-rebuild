Every coach on our floor brings one of two things — usually both: real competitive playing experience, and the patience and training to teach it to a kid who's just starting out. We built our staff on purpose to mix former collegiate athletes and elite competitive players with trained educators and standout recreational coaches, because a great athlete isn't automatically a great teacher, and the best programs need both.

## Baseball & softball

**Joe Palmeri**, baseball pitching coach, holds three collegiate pitching records from his time at Manhattanville College (class of 2015) and has coached as an assistant pitching coach at his alma mater.

**Alex Tejera**, head softball coach, was a scholarship athlete at Fordham University and a four-year letter winner. He's coached at the high school level since 2002, including as head softball coach at Rye High School since 2004.

**Debra Mazzeo**, softball pitching coach, pitched varsity at Bloomfield High School before pitching at County College of Morris and New England College.

**Micki Secoda**, softball pitching coach, is a senior pitcher at Mercy University in Dobbs Ferry with roughly 200 college innings and a decade of pitching experience.

**Ella Utschig**, softball pitcher and coach, pitches for the University of Pennsylvania and was an All-State, League Pitcher of the Year selection in high school before competing with the Rhode Island Thunder on the 16U PGF Nationals circuit.

## Volleyball

**Lydia Aryee**, volleyball coach, plays for the University of Mount Saint Vincent after competing for Downstate, with prior coaching experience at Shrub Oak Athletic Club and area summer camps.

**Coach Andrea Classen** leads the ICS Boys Volleyball Program. She grew up playing in South Africa, started the U14 South African National Team as starting setter at age 11, and later led the ICS JVB team to a CYO Championship title.

## Field hockey & lacrosse

**Melissa Goncalves**, field hockey and lacrosse coach, was a Division III athlete at Manhattanville College (class of 2013) and a two-time First Team All-Conference honoree in lacrosse, finishing her career with 203 points.

**Pam LaLonde**, field hockey and lacrosse coach, spent 18 years as a PE teacher and 20 years coaching. She co-captained the lacrosse team at Oneonta State College, earned All-SUNYAC First Team honors, broke the school's draw control record, and was named team MVP.

**Patrick Young**, lacrosse coach, has more than seven years of youth and varsity high school coaching experience. He captained his team at Manchester High School West (NH), played Division III at Plymouth State University, and is a US Lacrosse Certified Official.

## Multi-sport & strength

**Will Green**, owner and head trainer of WGPT, brings 22-plus years in the fitness industry and 36 years around basketball, including 22 years coaching AAU and youth leagues, with additional expertise in pre- and post-natal fitness and sport-specific training.

**Jamal Fernandez**, multi-sport coach, is a New Rochelle native with 21 years playing football, basketball, rugby, and track, and 13 years coaching. He currently plays rugby for the White Plains Warpigs.

**Coach Greene**, strength and conditioning coach, is the Head Strength & Conditioning Coach at Dream Academy High School and holds CSCS and USAW-1 certifications along with an Exercise Science degree from Lehman College.

## Academics

**Nicole Goodrich**, Director of Remote Learning, has 28-plus years in teaching and program direction. She founded the early childhood program Nic of Time (2001–2020) and previously worked as a Pre-K assistant teacher at Our Lady of Perpetual Help School.
