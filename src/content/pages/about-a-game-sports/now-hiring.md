We're always looking for coaches and staff who can hold both halves of what we do here: real skill instruction, and the patience to teach an 8-year-old who's having a rough day. If that sounds like you, we want to hear from you.

## Current openings

**Instructors** (part-time and full-time) in:
- Baseball
- Softball
- Soccer
- Lacrosse
- Pre-K

**Support staff:**
- Front Desk
- Birthday Party & Special Event Assistants

## How to apply

Email your resume to **darin@agamesports.net**, or reach out through our [contact form](/contact/) and let us know which role you're interested in. We'll follow up to talk through experience, availability, and where you'd fit best on the floor.

Don't see your exact sport or role listed but think you'd be a fit anyway? Reach out — we're building a staff for the long haul, not just filling a slot.
