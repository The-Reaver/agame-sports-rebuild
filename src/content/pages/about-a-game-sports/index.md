Walk in on a Tuesday afternoon and you'll see it: a 6-year-old taking their first swing next to a travel team running drills two courts over. That's A-Game on purpose. We built this place so a kid who's never picked up a lacrosse stick and a kid gunning for a travel roster spot can both find their level here, under the same roof, in New Rochelle.

## Why we started this

A-Game Sports opened in 2013 with a simple bet: that a facility built around kids — not just around sports — would earn a family's trust for the long haul. More than a decade later, that's still the whole plan.

> "At A-Game Sports we don't just make better athletes, we make better people."

We fuse hard work and fun on purpose, because we've watched what happens when a program picks only one. All-fun programs don't build anything that lasts. All-drills programs burn kids out before they find out if they even like the sport. We aim for both, every session.

## What we're actually trying to teach

Underneath the drills, we're coaching for things that outlast the season: determination, discipline, preparation, teamwork, sportsmanship, and personal accountability. Every coach on our floor is teaching your kid how to compete — and just as importantly, how to lose, adjust, and come back better.

We built our programs so that athletic opportunity isn't gated by how naturally gifted, well-funded, or motivated a kid already is when they walk in the door. If your kid wants to try, we want to coach them.

## Who's actually coaching

Our staff mixes former collegiate athletes and elite competitive players with trained educators and standout recreational coaches — people who know both the sport and how to talk to an 8-year-old having a rough day. Meet the full roster on our [staff page](/about-a-game-sports/staff/).

## Come see it for yourself

The best way to understand A-Game is to stand in the building. [Book a tour](/contact/), or just call — we'll walk you through the space and help you figure out where your kid fits.
