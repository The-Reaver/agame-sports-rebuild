Boys and girls, first-timers through advanced dodgers — A-Game's fall lacrosse lineup is the deepest on our schedule, organized by level and gender so every player lands in the right group, indoors and outdoors while weather allows.

## This fall's programs

- **Boys Intro To Lacrosse Level 1** and **Girls Intro To Lacrosse Level 1** — first exposure to the sport's fundamentals
- **Boys Rising Lacrosse Stars Level 2** and **Girls Rising Lacrosse Stars Level 2** — building on first-year basics
- **Boys Advanced Dodging & Shooting Level 3** and **Girls Advanced Dodging & Shooting Level 3** — higher-level skill and game work
- **Girls Outdoor Intro to Lacrosse Level 1** — fundamentals, played outdoors
- **Girls Outdoor Rising Lacrosse Stars Level 2** and **Girls 6 Week Outdoor Rising Lacrosse Stars Level 2** — level 2 skills, outdoor format
- **Boys Fundamentals of Lacrosse Program** and **Girls Fundamentals of Lacrosse Program**
- **Boys Intermediate Dodging and Shooting**
- **Girls Shooting and Draw Control Lacrosse Program**
- **Boys Advanced Dodging and Defense**
- **Boys & Girls Lacrosse Goalie Training**
- **Boys Sharp Shooter Series Level 4**

## Ages, pricing, and exact dates

Fall program dates, age breakdowns, and pricing are set closer to each season and can shift year to year — call us at **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to get the current fall schedule and reserve a spot before a program fills.

## Looking for more individual attention?

If your player wants dedicated one-on-one time instead of — or alongside — a fall program, take a look at our **[1-on-1 lessons](/lacrosse/1-on-1-lessons/)** or **[small group lessons](/lacrosse/lacrosse-small-group-lessons/)**.
