Spring and summer aren't the only seasons that matter. A-Game's fall soccer lineup keeps players sharp as outdoor fields empty out, so nobody starts next spring from scratch.

## This fall's programs

- **Soccer ABC's** — introductory instruction for players new to the game
- **Basic Soccer Skills Level 2** — building on first-touch fundamentals
- **Advanced Soccer Skills** — instruction for more experienced players
- **6 Week Outdoor Basic Soccer Skills Level 2** — outdoor version while weather allows
- **Outdoor Basic Soccer Skills Level 2** — outdoor fundamentals training
- **Outdoor Advanced Soccer Skills** — outdoor advanced-level training
- **Little Athletes Micro Soccer** — soccer introduction for our youngest players

Each series runs as its own block of sessions; a player can register for one, or stack a few depending on age, level, and what they want to work on.

## Ages, pricing, and exact dates

Fall series dates, age breakdowns, and pricing are set closer to each season and can shift year to year — call us at **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to get the current fall schedule and reserve a spot before a series fills.

## Looking for more individual attention?

If your player wants dedicated one-on-one time instead of (or alongside) a fall series, take a look at our **[1-on-1 lessons](/soccer-programs/soccer-1-on-1-lessons/)** or **[small group lessons](/soccer-programs/soccer-small-group-lessons/)**.
