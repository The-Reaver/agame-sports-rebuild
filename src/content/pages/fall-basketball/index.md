Once the summer camp schedule wraps, our fall basketball lineup keeps players moving — from skill-focused training sessions to live scrimmage reps against other kids their age.

## What runs in the fall

Our fall basketball offerings span instructional scrimmages, focused skill-building training, a dedicated girls-only program, and academy-style sessions that mix drills with live play. Age groupings, current pricing, and exact start dates are set closer to each season — call **(914) 278-9477** for what's currently open.

For teams and families who want a single competitive day rather than a multi-week program, our outdoor **[Nothing But Net 3-on-3 Tournament](/fall-basketball/fall-outdoor-nothing-but-net-3-on-3-basketball-tournament/)** runs each fall as a standalone bracket event.

## Signing up

Fall programs fill by age group and skill level, so the fastest way to find the right fit is a call — we'll walk you through what's currently running and where your player belongs.
