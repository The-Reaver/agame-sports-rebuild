Our outdoor Nothing But Net tournament is a single-day, bracket-style event — sign up solo and we'll place you on a team, or register a full team of up to 4 players and play together from game one.

## Divisions

Teams compete in three age divisions — **9U**, **11U**, and **13U** — based on age as of that season's cutoff date. Each division caps at 8 teams, so spots go fast once registration opens.

## How it plays out

Every team is guaranteed at least **3 games**. Games run **15 minutes of running time or to 15 points**, whichever comes first, with baskets worth 2 or 3 points depending on distance. After pool play, the top two teams in each division meet in a championship game — seeding and tiebreakers come down to win-loss record, then point differential. Champions in each division take home trophies.

## Registration

Sign up as an individual for **$70** and we'll assign you to a team, or register a full team of up to 4 players for **$225**. This tournament has most recently run at Temple Israel in New Rochelle — call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's exact date and location. Full game rules go out a few days before tip-off.
