A-Game has been in New Rochelle since 2013, and our specials page has always leaned into that — anniversary discounts on programs, seasonal price breaks on lessons and rentals, and a standing discount for military families. Specific offers rotate by season, so treat what's below as a snapshot of the kind of specials we run, and call to confirm what's active right now.

## Always available

**Veteran discount** — active duty service members, veterans, and their immediate families receive **15% off all lessons and programs**, year-round, no expiration.

## Specials that rotate seasonally

Past and recent specials have included things like:

- **Half Price Saturdays** — 60-minute lessons in select sports discounted to $70/hour instead of $140/hour, limited to newly booked lessons.
- **Rental discounts** on infield time and batting tunnels during set windows.
- **Summer lesson discounts** — 50% off private and small-group baseball/softball and strength & conditioning lessons during set summer weekday hours.
- **Birthday party discounts** on our Classic Package during select summer dates.
- **Anniversary program discounts** — stacking discounts (for example, 25% off a second program, 50% off a third) on select winter programs or summer camps booked together.
- **Flash sales** tied to specific short windows, like an extra camp week at a discount if you call within a promotional period.

Because these come and go — some are tied to a specific date range, a promo code, or a call-to-book requirement rather than online booking — we're not listing exact current terms here to avoid pointing you to something that's already expired.

## Get the current list

Call **(914) 278-9477** or [contact us](/contact/) and ask what's currently running — we'll walk you through any active discounts for the sport, camp, or program you're looking at. You can also check our [calendar](/calendar/) for what's in session right now.
