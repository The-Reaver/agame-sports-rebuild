Small group lessons bring competitive energy. But when a player has one specific thing holding them back, nothing replaces a coach watching only them for the full session. Our field hockey 1-on-1 lessons are built around each player's specific needs and desired areas of improvement — an instructor breaks the mechanics down to their simplest form and works from there.

## What a session covers

Instructors work across the full range of field hockey skills, breaking fundamentals down while keeping the session engaging — because a lesson that isn't fun doesn't stick.
