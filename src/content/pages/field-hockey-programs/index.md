Outdoor season is short. A-Game's indoor turf means your player doesn't have to wait for spring to keep working — cutting, trapping, and shooting stay part of the routine through the coldest months of the year.

## How players train with us

**1-on-1 lessons** give one instructor's full attention to one player's specific area of improvement — a hesitant trap, a weak drag flick, footwork that needs cleaning up. **Small group lessons** keep that individualized coaching but add two or three teammates for competitive reps. **Team training** brings a full roster in to work situational play or condition together ahead of — or during — a season.

Looking for a fall option? **[Fall Field Hockey](/fall-field-hockey/)** runs a lineup of level-based programs so players keep building skills once the outdoor season wraps.

## Not sure where to start?

New to structured field hockey training? A 1-on-1 or small group lesson is the fastest way for a coach to see where your player is at and build a plan around it. Already rostered on a team? Ask us about team training built around your season schedule.
