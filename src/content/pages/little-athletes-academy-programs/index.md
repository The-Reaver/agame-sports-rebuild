Big-kid energy can be a lot for a two-year-old. Little Athletes Academy is A-Game's answer: classes and lessons sized for toddlers and preschoolers, taught by coaches who know the difference between coaching a 4-year-old and coaching a 14-year-old — and are good at both.

## How families use this program

**[Customized Playgroups](/little-athletes-academy-programs/customized-playgroups/)** let you build a weekly class around your child's age and interests — soccer, golf, movement, basketball, or a multi-sport mix. **[Sports Socialization & Instruction](/little-athletes-academy-programs/sports-socialization-and-instruction/)** offers private 1-on-1 lessons for kids who do best with an instructor's full attention before joining a group.

In summer, **[Super Summer Saturdays](/little-athletes-academy-programs/super-summer-saturdays/)** runs a weekly Saturday class. Every fall, **[Fall Little Athletes](/fall-little-athletes/)** opens up our widest range of classes, from a Mommy & Me session for babies to Pre-K flag football for kindergarten-bound kids.

## Not sure where to start?

If your child is nervous around groups or brand new to organized activity, a private 1-on-1 lesson is a gentle way in. If they're ready to be around other kids their age, a playgroup or seasonal class is the better first step — the same building where their older siblings train.
