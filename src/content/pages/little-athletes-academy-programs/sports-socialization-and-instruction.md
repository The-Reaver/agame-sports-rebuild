Some kids aren't ready to jump into a group class, and that's completely normal. A 1-on-1 lesson gives a child ages 4 or 5 a coach's undivided attention, working on **soccer**, **baseball**, **flag football**, or **basketball** at a pace that fits them specifically.

## What it builds

Beyond sport-specific skills, these sessions help develop fine motor skills, coordination, and balance — and give a child the chance to build real self-esteem before stepping into a group setting. It's a natural fit for a child who's hesitant in groups, or for parents who want a gentle, low-pressure introduction to organized sports before school age.

Many families use 1-on-1 lessons as a bridge — once a child is comfortable, they transition into one of our group [playgroups](/little-athletes-academy-programs/customized-playgroups/) at their own pace.
