No summer heat, no rained-out fields — Super Summer Saturdays runs entirely indoors, in our air-conditioned facility, one morning a week from **July 11 through August 8**.

## Pick a class that fits your child's age

- **Micro Soccer** — ages 3–5, 9:15–10:00am
- **Basketball on the Court** — ages 4–5, 9:15–10:00am or 10:00–10:45am
- **Multi Sport Minis** — ages 1½–3, 10:00–10:45am
- **Multi Sport Madness** — ages 3–5, 11:00–11:45am

Every class is coach-led and built for the age group it serves, so a toddler in Multi Sport Minis and a kindergarten-bound kid in Micro Soccer are both getting instruction pitched exactly where they are.

## Cost and booking

The session runs **$140**. Call **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to reserve your child's spot — see our [policies page](/policies/) for cancellation and makeup details.
