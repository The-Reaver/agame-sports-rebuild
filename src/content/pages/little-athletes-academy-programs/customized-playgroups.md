Not every family's week fits a fixed class schedule. Customized Playgroups let you set the day, the time, and the sport focus, and we build a small group class around it — with kids ages 2 to 5 who fit the same window.

## How it works

Each session runs **once a week for 10 weeks**, with groups of **4 to 12 children**, so there's enough energy in the room for real play without anyone getting lost in the crowd. Weekday classes run between 9:00am and 4:00pm; weekend classes run 9:00am to 12:00pm.

## Choose the focus

Playgroups can be built around **multi-sport play**, **soccer**, **golf**, **movement and field games**, or **basketball** — whatever fits your child's interest at this age. Coaches keep the format playful and low-pressure; the goal at this age is joy and comfort in a group, not competition.

## Booking

Call **(914) 278-9477** to set up a playgroup that matches your schedule and your child's interests — pricing is **$250 for the full 10-week session**, about $25 per class.
