A scheduled class works great until it doesn't — a friend group that only overlaps on Sunday mornings, a small travel squad that needs extra reps before playoffs, a homeschool pod that wants PE built into the week. Customized Programs exist for exactly that gap: you set the schedule, we build the training around it.

## Sports available

Baseball, softball, soccer, lacrosse, football, field hockey, volleyball, basketball, and strength & conditioning — across all skill levels, from first-timers to competitive players.

## Formats and pricing

Choose your group size — pricing is per person, per one-hour session, and drops the more sessions you book:

| Format | 1 session | 5 sessions | 10 sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-on-1 | $140 | $115/session | $100/session |
| 2-on-1 | $85/person | $72.50/person | $60/person |
| 3-on-1 | $73.33/person | $61.66/person | $52/person |
| 4-on-1 | $61.25/person | $52.25/person | $44/person |

Group formats are more cost-effective per player than solo lessons while still keeping real individual coaching attention — a coach working with 2, 3, or 4 players still sees every player's mechanics, every rep.

## How it works

You bring the group and the general goal; we work with you to set the schedule and match a coach to the focus area. There's no fixed curriculum to opt into — sessions are built around what your specific group needs.
