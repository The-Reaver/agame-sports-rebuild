## Participant Release Form

Every A-Game participant needs a signed release form on file before their first session, lesson, or camp day.

- **Who fills it out:** A parent or legal guardian
- **How to get it:** Download the PDF from our site or pick one up at the facility
- **Where it goes:** Submit the signed form to A-Game Sports by email or in person — keep a copy for your own records

## Medical Form (required for summer camps)

Summer campers need an additional medical form completed by a **physician**.

- **Deadline:** Must be on file by **May 1** for that summer's camp season
- **Submit to:** [info@agamesports.net](mailto:info@agamesports.net), attention Camp Director Nicole Goodrich
- **Important:** Campers cannot be admitted to camp until this form is on file — please don't wait until move-in week to submit it

## How to submit

1. Complete your program or camp registration online.
2. Fill out the release form (and medical form, if you're registering for camp).
3. Keep a personal copy of each form.
4. Email your signed forms to us, or drop them off in person at 856 Main St, New Rochelle.

Questions about which forms apply to your child's program? Call us at **(914) 278-9477** — we're happy to walk through it before you register.
