Between Little Athletes classes, sport-specific training, strength & conditioning, and seasonal programs, A-Game runs a full weekly calendar of scheduled sessions — well over a hundred class slots across a typical week, organized by day and time with the date range each session runs.

## What's on it

The calendar covers Little Athletes programs (ages 3–5), baseball at every skill level, basketball training, field hockey, lacrosse, soccer, softball, flag football, volleyball, and strength & conditioning — each listing its day, time, and the weeks it runs.

## The calendar changes seasonally

Because sessions run in blocks tied to the school calendar (fall, winter, spring, summer), the specific programs, days, and times on our calendar shift throughout the year. Rather than reproduce a schedule here that could be out of date by the time you read it, the most reliable way to see what's currently running — and whether there's an open spot — is to call us directly.

## Check current availability

Call **(914) 278-9477** or [contact us](/contact/) and tell us your kid's age and sport of interest — we'll walk you through what's on the calendar right now and where there's room. You can also ask about our [Customized Programs](/customized-programs/) if nothing on the standing calendar lines up with your schedule.
