Energia Health and Fitness runs a full slate of personal and group training for adults inside A-Game's conditioning room — so you can get a real workout in without leaving New Rochelle.

## Weekly classes

- **AM Express** — Mondays & Wednesdays, 6:30am–7:15am. An early-morning express workout class.
- **Beachbody** — Wednesdays, 8:30am–9:30am (1 hour). Total-body strength training; walk-ins welcome.
- **Strength + Stretch** — Mondays, 9:15am–10:15am (1 hour). 30 minutes of core training paired with a 30-minute stretch; walk-ins welcome.
- **Cardio Kickboxing** — Saturdays, 8:30am–9:30am (1 hour). A 60-minute kickboxing workout; walk-ins welcome.

Class days, times, and pricing (for example, AM Express has run as an 8-class monthly series) are set seasonally — call **(914) 278-9477** or email **lisa@energianewyork.com** to confirm what's currently running before you show up.

## Health coaching

Beyond the workouts, Energia's nationally certified Health Coaches meet one-on-one to help you make lasting lifestyle changes — built on the idea that most of "health-care" is actually "self-care."

## Getting started

Most classes welcome walk-ins, but call ahead or email Lisa Greco directly to confirm the current schedule, pricing, and space before your first visit.
