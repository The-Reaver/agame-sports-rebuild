Some players learn fastest with a little competition in the room. Small group lessons keep the same individual attention as our 1-on-1 sessions but bring 2 to 4 players together, so every rep has a little extra push behind it.

## What a session covers

Groups train the same core skills as our 1-on-1 sessions — **throwing**, **catching**, **running**, **blocking**, **route running**, and **defense** — with drills built to create friendly competition between the players in the group.

## Group sizes and pricing

All small group sessions run **60 minutes**. Price depends on how many players are in your group:

| Package | 2 players | 3 players | 4 players |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 session | $170 | $220 | $245 |
| 5 sessions | $725 | $925 | $1,045 |
| 10 sessions | $1,200 | $1,560 | $1,760 |

Bring your own group of friends or teammates, or ask us to help place your player with others at a similar level. See our [policies page](/policies/) for cancellation and makeup terms.
