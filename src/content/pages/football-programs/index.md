Flag football strips out the collisions and keeps everything else that makes the sport worth playing — routes, reads, throws, and the moment a defender rips a flag at the last second. A-Game's indoor space means that development doesn't stop when the weather turns.

## How players train with us

**1-on-1 lessons** put a football-minded instructor's full attention on one player, working through throwing, catching, running, blocking, route running, or defense — whatever that player needs most. **Small group lessons** keep that same individual coaching but bring 2 to 4 players together for extra competitive energy.

Players ready for a bigger commitment can try out for **[Travel Flag Football](/football-programs/travel-flag-football/)**, a competitive co-ed team that plays under NFL Flag rules. And every fall, **[Fall Football](/fall-football/)** runs a full slate of scrimmages and instructional leagues across grade levels.

## Not sure where to start?

If your player is new to flag football, a 1-on-1 or small-group lesson is the fastest way for a coach to see where they're at and build a plan from there. Chasing a competitive roster spot? Ask us about tryouts for the travel program.
