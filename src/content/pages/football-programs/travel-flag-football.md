Our travel program is built for players who've outgrown recreational flag football and want a real competitive season. Teams carry a roster of **10 players**, split into **10U**, **12U**, and **14U** divisions, and play under official **NFL Flag rules**.

## What to expect

This is a step up from lessons and small groups — coaches run the team like a real travel program, with the standards and structure that come with it, while still keeping the tight-knit, everybody-matters feel of a small roster. Players who make a team travel to compete against other top programs, not just play out a rec-league schedule.

## Tryouts

Tryouts are held each year and are free to attend, but require registration in advance. Exact tryout dates, location, and age cutoffs are set fresh for each season — call **(914) 278-9477** to confirm this year's tryout schedule and eligibility date, and to ask about current registration pricing.

Review our [policies page](/policies/) for medical form requirements before tryouts.
