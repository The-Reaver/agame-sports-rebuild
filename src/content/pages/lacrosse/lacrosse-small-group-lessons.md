Small group lacrosse lessons are designed for 2 to 4 players who share specific needs and desired areas of improvement. Instructors work on shooting, passing, stick handling, goaltending, face-offs, or defense — with the built-in competition between players keeping the energy up while everyone gets real, individual coaching.

## Session length and group size

All small group lessons run **60 minutes**, with groups of **2 to 4 players**. Bring your own training partners, or ask us about matching your player with others working on similar skills.
