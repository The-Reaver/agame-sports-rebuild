Outdoor lacrosse has a season. Development doesn't have to. A-Game's indoor space keeps players working on shooting, passing, stick handling, goaltending, face-offs, and defense straight through the New Rochelle offseason.

## How players train with us

**1-on-1 lessons** put one instructor's full attention on one player's specific area of improvement. **Small group lessons** keep that individualized coaching while adding two or three teammates for competitive reps. **Team training** brings your full roster in for season prep or focused work on a specific part of the game.

Looking for a fall option? **[Fall Lacrosse](/fall-lacrosse/)** runs a full lineup of level-based boys' and girls' programs so players keep developing once the outdoor season ends.

## Not sure where to start?

New to structured lacrosse training? A 1-on-1 or small group lesson is the fastest way for a coach to see where your player is at and build a plan around it. Already rostered on a team? Ask us about team training built around your season schedule.
