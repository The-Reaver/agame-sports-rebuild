## Refunds

All purchases are final — this includes lessons, programs, camps, birthday parties, and every other service we offer. We ask you to take a close look at what you're booking before you check out, and call us first if you're unsure it's the right fit.

## Camp cancellations

Once a camp session is purchased, it can't be refunded or credited to your account — with one exception: if a doctor confirms in writing that your camper can't participate, we'll issue an account credit (not a refund) valid for one year toward another A-Game service. Camp fees can't be converted into credit for lessons or other non-camp programs.

## Multi-day and multi-week pricing

Our discounted multi-day and multi-week rates apply when you choose all of your days or weeks at the time you register. Adding days later is billed at the standard single-day or single-week rate — it won't retroactively apply the bundle discount.

## Lesson package expiration

- **5-lesson packages** are valid for 6 months from purchase.
- **10-lesson and 20-lesson packages** are valid for 12 months from purchase.

Unused sessions are forfeited once a package expires, so we'd rather see you use them — talk to your instructor about scheduling if you're falling behind.

## Cancelling a booked lesson

If you need to cancel a scheduled lesson, we need **at least 24 hours' notice**. Cancellations inside that window are forfeited — the same way most instructor time works when it's held just for your session.

## Instructor requests

We'll do everything we can to place your child with the instructor you request, but we can't guarantee availability every time. If your preferred instructor isn't available for a given session, that session isn't refunded — we'll work with you to find the best available match.

## Weather and outdoor programs

Cold weather is never a reason we cancel a program outright — we plan for it. If a session truly can't run and can't be rescheduled, you'll receive an account credit toward a future session rather than losing it.

## Makeup sessions

Makeups and credits are issued in two situations only: a documented medical reason (doctor's note required), or a cancellation on our end due to weather or a facility issue. We're not able to offer makeups for missed sessions outside of those two cases.

## What we require before your child participates

- A completed **release form**, signed by a parent or legal guardian
- For summer camps, a **physician-completed medical form** and, for Little Athletes programs, current **immunization records**
- Toddler programs require campers to be **potty trained**

See our [Release Forms page](/release-forms/) for how to submit these.

## Food and allergies

Families provide their own water, lunch, and snacks. Because our facility is **not peanut-free**, we can't accommodate peanut-butter-specific requests — if your child has a nut allergy, please send any required **EpiPen** with clear instructions for our staff.

## Extended day registration

Extended day care requires a **one-week minimum commitment** and **one week's advance notice** to register.
