You don't age out of wanting a real touch on the ball. A-Game's indoor turf field hosts adult soccer in the evenings — league play through A-Game Sports, plus non-league competitive options if you're looking for something less structured.

## How it works

Games run on our turfed indoor field, built to handle instructional sessions and small-sided competitive play alike. Whether you're joining a league or organizing a group of your own, sessions are scheduled within our regular hours so you can get a run in on a weeknight without the field being at the mercy of the weather.

## Bringing your own group

If you'd rather book the field outright for your group — a regular pickup run, a company game, a one-off — that's handled through **[Facility Rentals](/birthdays-events-rentals/facility-rentals/)**, which has current rental rates.
