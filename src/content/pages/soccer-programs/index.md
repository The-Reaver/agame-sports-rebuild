Outdoor soccer stops for winter. Ours doesn't. A-Game's indoor turf field means your player keeps working on first touch, footwork, and game sense straight through the New Rochelle offseason, instead of picking it back up cold every spring.

## How players train with us

**1-on-1 lessons** put a coach's full attention on one player's specific need — cleaning up a first touch, sharpening finishing, building a keeper's positioning. **Small group lessons** keep that same individual coaching but add the energy of training next to two or three teammates. **Team training** brings a full roster in for the situational work that's hard to drill without a group — build-up play, pressing triggers, set pieces.

Grown-up players haven't been forgotten either: our **[Adult Soccer](/soccer-programs/adult-soccer/)** program puts the same turfed field to work for evening leagues and pickup-style competitive games. And every fall, **[Fall Soccer](/fall-soccer/)** runs a full lineup of seasonal series to keep young players sharp between spring and summer seasons.

## Not sure where to start?

New to structured training? A 1-on-1 or small-group lesson is the fastest way for a coach to see where your player's game actually is and build a plan from there. Already rostered on a team? Ask us about a team training block built around your season.
