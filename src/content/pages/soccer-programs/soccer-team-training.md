Some things are hard to drill without a full team on the field — build-up play, pressing triggers, defensive shape, set pieces. A-Game's team training sessions bring your whole roster onto our indoor turf to work on exactly that, whether you're getting a team ready for a season or sharpening things up mid-season.

## What a session covers

Sessions are customized to your team — we can build a block around one specific phase of the game, or fold in strength and conditioning alongside soccer skill work. Either way, the goal is the same: get every player on the roster more reps, and build team camaraderie while doing it.

## Who this is for

Team training is built for rostered teams — school teams, club teams, or rec teams looking for extra indoor practice time during the off-season.

Pricing and session length depend on your team's size and the focus of the session — call us at **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to talk through what your team needs and get a quote. See our [policies page](/policies/) for cancellation and makeup session terms.
