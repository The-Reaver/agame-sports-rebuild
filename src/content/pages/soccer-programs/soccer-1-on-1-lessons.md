Small group lessons bring competitive energy. But when one specific thing is holding a player back — a first touch that needs cleaning up, finishing that's inconsistent, footwork on the ball — nothing replaces a coach watching only them, for the full session.

## What a session covers

Instructors work across **shooting**, **dribbling**, **passing**, **defense**, and **goalkeeping**. Every lesson starts with the player's specific goals, then breaks the mechanics down to their simplest form so the fix actually sticks.
