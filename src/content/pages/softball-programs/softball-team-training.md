Some things are hard to drill without a full team on the field — relays, defensive alignment, situational at-bats. A-Game's team training sessions bring your whole roster into our indoor facility to work on exactly that, whether you're getting ready for a season or sharpening things up mid-season.

## What a session covers

Sessions are customized to your team — we can build a block around one specific part of the game, or fold in strength and conditioning alongside softball skill work. Either way, the goal is the same: get every player on the roster more reps, and build team camaraderie while doing it.

## Who this is for

Team training is built for rostered teams — travel squads like **[Westchester Heat](/softball-programs/heat-softball-teams/)**, school teams, or rec league teams looking for extra indoor practice time during the off-season.

## Booking

Pricing and session length depend on your team's size and the focus of the session — call us at **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to talk through what your team needs and get a quote. See our [policies page](/policies/) for cancellation and makeup session terms.
