Winter doesn't have to mean rust. A-Game's indoor cages, pitching machines, and turf mean your player keeps hitting, throwing, and fielding straight through the New Rochelle offseason — not just during the warm months everyone else gets.

## How players train with us

**1-on-1 lessons** zero in on one player's specific weak spot — a swing that needs rebuilding, glove work, a windmill motion that's lost its rhythm. Bring a sibling or a couple of teammates for **small group lessons** and keep that same coaching focus with some competitive energy mixed in. When it's time to work as a unit, **team training** brings the whole roster in for relays, defensive alignment, and situational at-bats — reps that are hard to get with just one or two players on the field.

For players chasing something more competitive, our **[Westchester Heat travel program](/softball-programs/heat-softball-teams/)** fields tournament-level 10U and 12U teams for players ready for a bigger commitment. And every fall, **[Fall Softball](/fall-softball/)** keeps the swing and the arm sharp between summer and next spring.

## Not sure where to start?

If your player is new to structured training, start with a 1-on-1 or small-group lesson — it's the fastest way for a coach to see where they're at and build a plan from there. If they're already rostered on a team, ask us about team training blocks built around your season schedule.
