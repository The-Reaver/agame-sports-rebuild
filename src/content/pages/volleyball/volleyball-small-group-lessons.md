Bring a sibling, friend, or a couple of teammates and train together — small group lessons keep the coach's attention personal while adding the competitive push of working alongside other players. Every session runs **60 minutes**, and groups range from **2 to 4 players**.

## Pricing by group size

Pricing is per group, not per player, and packages bring the per-session cost down.

| Group size | 1 session | 5 sessions | 10 sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 players | $170 | $725 | $1,200 |
| 3 players | $220 | $925 | $1,560 |
| 4 players | $245 | $1,045 | $1,760 |

## What a session covers

Instructors work on technical fundamentals — serving, setting, passing, and hitting — adjusted for the group's mix of beginners through advanced players, with an emphasis on refining skills through repetition and light competition.
