Group lessons bring competitive energy. But when a player has one specific thing holding them back — a serve that won't clear the net consistently, footwork on approach, or arm swing timing on a spike — nothing replaces a coach watching only them, for the full session.

## What a session covers

Instructors work across core skills: **serving**, **setting**, **passing**, and **hitting**. Every lesson starts with the player's specific goals, then breaks the mechanics down to their simplest form so the fix actually sticks — for beginners just learning the game and advanced players refining it.
