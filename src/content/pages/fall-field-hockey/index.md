Outdoor field hockey season doesn't have to be the only time your player gets real reps. A-Game's fall lineup keeps skills sharp with programs organized by level, so every player — from a first-timer to an advanced athlete — trains at the right pace.

## This fall's programs

- **Girls Intro to Field Hockey** — first exposure to the sport's fundamentals
- **Girls Field Hockey Basic Skills Level 2** — building on first-year basics
- **Girls Advanced Field Hockey Training Level 3** — higher-level skill and game work
- **Girls Outdoor Intro to Field Hockey** — fundamentals, played outdoors
- **Girls Outdoor Field Hockey Basic Skills Level 2** — level 2 skills, outdoor format
- **Outdoor Field Hockey Skills, Drills & Scrimmages** — skill work combined with live scrimmage play

## Ages, pricing, and exact dates

Fall program dates, age breakdowns, and pricing are set closer to each season and can shift year to year — call us at **(914) 278-9477** or use the contact form to get the current fall schedule and reserve a spot before a program fills.

## Looking for more individual attention?

If your player wants dedicated one-on-one time instead of — or alongside — a fall program, take a look at our **[1-on-1 lessons](/field-hockey-programs/field-hockey-1-on-1-lessons/)** or **[small group lessons](/field-hockey-programs/field-hockey-small-group-lessons/)**.
